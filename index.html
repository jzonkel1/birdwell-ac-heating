About us

Keeping Corpus Christi comfortable, one home at a time.

Birdwell A/C & Heating is owned and run by Jason and Tia Birdwell. Jason brings more than twenty years of hands-on HVAC experience to every job. Tia runs the office, which is why you get a real person on the phone and a text back when you have a question.

We opened our doors in 2018 with a simple idea: everyone deserves comfort in their home, no matter the season or the situation. Since then we’ve grown into a full crew of licensed technicians, earned an A+ from the Better Business Bureau, and been voted a Community’s Choice winner by the people we work for. We’re proud members of the United Corpus Christi Chamber of Commerce, BNI and the Padre Island Business Association.
